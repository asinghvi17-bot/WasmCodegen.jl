The packages, unchanged

      
ImageFiltering, ImageCore, ColorTypes, FixedPointNumbers and OffsetArrays from the General
        registry: __FUNCS__ Julia functions in __SIZE__ KB of WebAssembly, with __OBJECTS__ objects
        (the presets' tables, the padding styles) serialized into the module.

    

    

      
Separable or not

      
imfilter factors a 2-D kernel by its singular value decomposition: of rank one,
        it filters with two 1-D passes. The SVD is LAPACK's gesdd, from the runtime
        WasmCodegen compiles into the module, written in Julia.

    

    

      
Checked against Julia

      
On 29 cases (every preset, each border, color and gray), the module's pixels equal native
        Julia's, or differ by one level where a sum lands on a rounding boundary: the SVD's last
        bits differ from OpenBLAS's.
